ABOUT

A place to collect the parts of myself and put them somewhere.
Who I Am

I'm Apollo. I'm just a regular guy. Grew up in the early 2000's and played games. I don't really have any skills or anything. A lot of this site was vibe-coded, so I don't take credit for how nice it is.

Apollo

Apollo doesn't really mean anything. It's just a nickname I came up with. It makes me think of deep space. I know it's a god of some kind, but I can't remember which one. Just a nice name, and since I own the domain, I can make the alias whatever I want, so there.

Kosmic Kodex

I've had the same name online for over a decade: Redman. It got stale after a while, so I started to try and change it up. Kosmic Kodex is closer to me in terms of real identity, but I won't go into why. Are you starting to see the space theme yet?

What I Like

I like the Y2K era. That nostalgic vibe is my favorite setting. It's peaceful. I like to visit old sites from Web 1.0 to try to just imagine being there and finding these old sites. I grew up a little too late to see the cool internet, and as I get older, it seems like the best time to have been alive, but I guess no matter what age you are, that's always the case for everyone, right?

Why This Site Exists

I made this website just because I wanted to. I actually bought a domain for hosting an email address; however, when I had a domain that had no site associated, I figured I would give it a homepage, and I just kept going. I wanted email hosting to get away from google. Someday, aside from YouTube, I'll be fully google free.

Currently

I'm currently existing. Just trying to find a way to learn what I want to get where I want to be. I know that's vague. I want to be a programmer, but I can't sit still long enough to learn it. Guess I'll never be one with that attitude, and time is running out for me, but hey, a guy can dream. I'm working on fleshing this out a bit more, and then I'm gonna start replacing all my accounts associated with google.
